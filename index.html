Who We Are

Transforming Lives Across the North East

Joybringers is a community-led charity based in Newcastle dedicated to empowering children and young people aged 7–25. Through skills training, digital education, community events, and holistic wellbeing support, we help young people discover their potential and build bright futures.

We believe every young person — regardless of background — deserves access to opportunities, mentorship, and a community where they truly belong.

Our Programmes

What We Do

Hands-on programmes designed to build skills, confidence, and community for young people across the North East.

Digital Skills & Coding

Coding bootcamps, data analytics, game design, and web development for ages 7–25.

Creative Arts Training

Dance, music, visual arts, and crafts to inspire creativity and self-expression.

Skills for Life

CV writing, mock interviews, career guidance, and personal branding workshops.

Wellbeing & Financial Literacy

Mental health support, budgeting workshops, and self-care sessions.

Community Trips & Events

Visits to museums, parks, nature reserves, and fun community days out.

Food Bank & Family Support

Free surplus food collections and family support services across Newcastle and Gateshead.
